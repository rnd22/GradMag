BRIDGES 2027

B.R.I.D.G.E.S is the annual conference hosted by the SBU Graduate Arts & Sciences Magazine. The third conference will be held February 4–5, 2027, at the Student Activities Center.

Previous conferences brought together graduate scholars for research presentations, artistic work, and conversations across departments.

Submissions & Forms

Use the links below to apply. For help, contact sbugradmag@stonybrook.edu.
